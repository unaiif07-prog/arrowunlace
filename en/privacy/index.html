Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 (version 1.1: daily puzzle, reminder and English; NAIENZA brand) · Español

Who is responsible

Unai Fernandez Nuñez, publishing games under the NAIENZA brand. Contact: unaiif07@gmail.com.

What data the app uses

Your game. Completed levels, current attempt, stars, times, coins, hints, mascots, accessories, daily puzzles, streak, achievements and records (from version 1.1) and settings. They are stored only on your device: they are not sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). If you choose "Continue with Apple", Apple gives the app a user identifier and, if you allow it, your name. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google gives the app a user identifier and your profile name; we don't ask for your email address. The app keeps them only on your device to show which account you're signed in with. We don't send them to any server of ours or use them for anything else. Signing in doesn't copy or sync your game. You can play without signing in, as a guest.

Daily puzzle reminder (optional, from version 1.1). If you turn it on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules local reminders on your device. They are created and shown by your device: nothing is sent anywhere.

Nothing else. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party tools that collect data. It doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Who it is shared with

No one: the data never leaves your device. Signing in happens directly with Apple or Google, and is also governed by their privacy policies.

How to delete it

Account data: in the app, Settings › Account › "Delete account" erases your account identifier and name from the device.

  • To also remove the permission at Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › ArrowUnlace › Delete. If you remove it there first, the app notices and erases the account from the device.
  • To remove it at Google: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose ArrowUnlace and remove the connection.

Reminders: turn them off in Settings in the app, or in the iPhone notification settings.

Everything: uninstalling the app erases your game and all its data from the device.

Your rights

Since we keep no data about you outside your device, you can view, change or delete it yourself from the app. For any question, write to the contact email. If you live in the European Union, you can also file a complaint with your country's data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app changes the way it handles data, for example when adding advertising, we will update this policy before the change reaches the app and show the date.
