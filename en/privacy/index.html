Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 (version 1.2: Google AdMob ads and a new-version notice) · Español

Who is responsible

Unai Fernandez Nuñez, publishing games under the NAIENZA brand. Contact: unaiif07@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your game is saved only on your device. We have no servers and don't collect your data.
  • Up to version 1.1, the app has no ads. From version 1.2 it shows Google AdMob ads, and Google collects some data to show them, as explained below.
  • You decide about personalized ads: in the European Union, the UK and Switzerland, through Google's consent message; on iPhone, also through Apple's tracking permission.

What data the app uses

Your game. Completed levels, current attempt, stars, times, coins, hints, mascots, accessories, daily puzzles, streak, achievements and records (from version 1.1), whether you bought "Remove ads" and when the last ad between levels was shown (from 1.2), and settings. They are stored only on your device: they are not sent to any server of ours.

Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). If you choose "Continue with Apple", Apple gives the app a user identifier and, if you allow it, your name. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google gives the app a user identifier and your profile name; we don't ask for your email address. The app keeps them only on your device to show which account you're signed in with. We don't send them to any server of ours or use them for anything else, including ads. Signing in doesn't copy or sync your game. You can play without signing in, as a guest.

Daily puzzle reminder (optional, from version 1.1). If you turn it on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules local reminders on your device. They are created and shown by your device: nothing is sent anywhere.

The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, precise location, contacts or photos, and has no analytics of its own.

New-version notice (from version 1.2). At most once a day, the app asks Apple's App Store which version of ArrowUnlace is the latest, to let you know when there is a new one. It only sends the app's identifier: no data about you or your game. As with any connection, Apple receives the device's IP address.

Advertising (from version 1.2)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited (in the European Economic Area and Switzerland) and Google LLC (elsewhere). There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads, always optional: you only see them if you choose to get something in return (double a level's coins, keep going with a heart after losing, or a hint when you have none left).
  • Ads between levels, in moderation: never in the first levels or right after losing.

To show ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see each one and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects and sends to Google, encrypted:

  • the device's advertising identifier: on iPhone, only if you allow it through Apple's tracking prompt; on Android, the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings;
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location is estimated (country or city, not your exact location);
  • your interactions with ads (which ads you see and whether you tap them) and with the app (for example, when it opens);
  • technical and diagnostic data: device model, operating system, performance and crashes.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We don't receive your personal data: we only see aggregated ad statistics (how many are shown and what they earn).

Your choices:

  • Consent: in the European Union, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message at start-up, where you choose whether to accept personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app, under Settings › Ads and privacy › Privacy preferences. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • iPhone: the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't allow it, Google doesn't receive the advertising identifier. You can change it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase that removes the ads between levels for good. Rewarded ads stay optional.

The app is meant for all ages and asks Google only for ads suitable for everyone.

Purchases

For now the app has no real-money purchases. If a future version adds the "Remove ads" purchase, it will be paid through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own terms and privacy policies. We don't receive your payment details or your identity: the store only confirms to the app that the purchase exists, and the app will save that on your device. "Restore purchases" (in Settings) will bring it back on another device or after reinstalling.

Who it is shared with

Your game never leaves your device. From version 1.2, the advertising data described above is collected directly by Google (AdMob) through its SDK. Signing in happens directly with Apple or Google, the new-version check with Apple, and purchases, if any, with Apple or Google; each is also governed by its own privacy policy. We don't sell data or share it with anyone else.

How to delete it

Account data: in the app, Settings › Account › "Delete account" erases your account identifier and name from the device.

  • To also remove the permission at Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › ArrowUnlace › Delete. If you remove it there first, the app notices and erases the account from the device.
  • To remove it at Google: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose ArrowUnlace and remove the connection.

Advertising: you can reset or delete the advertising identifier in your phone's settings, and withdraw consent in the app (Settings › Ads and privacy › Privacy preferences). For the data Google keeps, you can exercise your rights with Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

Reminders: turn them off in Settings in the app, or in the iPhone notification settings.

Everything on the device: uninstalling the app erases your game and all its data from the device. If the "Remove ads" purchase is added in the future, it will stay with your Apple or Google account and come back with "Restore purchases".

Your rights

Since we keep no data about you outside your device, you can view, change or delete it yourself from the app. For any question, write to the contact email. If you live in the European Union, you can also file a complaint with your country's data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

The app is for all ages. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children, and we only ask Google for ads suitable for everyone.

Changes

If the app changes the way it handles data, we will update this policy before the change reaches the app and show the date.
