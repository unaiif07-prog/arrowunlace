Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Tap an arrow: it leaves in the direction its tip points, if nothing is in the way. If it bumps into another piece, you lose a heart. The level ends when every arrow is out.
What happens if I run out of hearts?
You can try again for free, with no waiting.
What are hints for?
The light bulb points to an arrow that can get out. You start with three.
How do I get stars?
Completing a level always gives one star. If you finish it within the level's times, you get two or three; tap the clock to see them. Time never makes you lose.
And coins?
You earn them by completing levels for the first time and by getting new stars, and from version 1.1 also with the daily puzzle. They buy accessories for your mascots; from version 1.1, each one also opens with the stars you earn in its region. Coins can't be bought with real money.
What is the daily puzzle? (from version 1.1)
An optional board every day, from level 10 on. Completing it gives 20 coins once a day and a stamp on the calendar. If you skip a day, you lose nothing.
How does the daily puzzle reminder work? (from version 1.1)
It's optional and off by default. Turn it on in Settings › Daily puzzle reminder or on the daily puzzle screen; the app then asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder arrives at 6 pm (your device's time) only on days when you haven't completed the puzzle yet. Your own device schedules it: we use no server. To stop it, turn it off in the app's Settings or in the iPhone notification settings.
What are the big challenges? (from version 1.1)
Much larger boards, separate from the regular levels, that you can zoom into and move around by dragging. You have five hearts. They open when you complete levels 20, 35, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100, and then two per region (at levels 125, 150, 175… up to 500): 24 in all.
How do I get more mascots?
Rizo is with you from the start. Each region has its own mascot, which you get by completing its 50 levels: Borla in Serene Meadow, Pluma in Misty Forest and, from version 1.1, eight more in the following regions. You never lose one, and accessories fit them all.
Can I change the language? (from version 1.1)
Yes: Settings › Language. "Automatic" follows your device: Spanish if it's in Spanish, English otherwise.
Can I turn the music off? (from version 1.1)
Yes: Settings › Music. Game sounds have their own switch (Sound). With the iPhone in silent mode the game makes no sound, and if you're listening to music or a podcast in another app, it keeps playing while you play.
What are the streak and achievements? (from version 1.1)
The streak counts the days in a row on which you complete at least one level; if it breaks, you lose nothing else. Achievements are medals for your progress, and records keep your best marks. You'll find them on the title screen, under "Achievements".
Why are there ads? (from version 1.2)
The game is free and ads help us keep making it. There are two kinds: rewarded ads, which you only see if you choose to double a level's coins, keep going with a heart after losing or get a hint when you have none left; and ads between levels, in moderation (never in the first ten levels, in your first session or right after losing). Offline, the game works the same and there are no ads.
How do I remove ads? (from version 1.2)
In Settings › Ads and privacy › "Remove ads". It's a one-time purchase that removes the ads between levels for good; rewarded ads are still there if you want them. If you change phones or reinstall the game, get your purchase back with "Restore purchases" in the same place.
How do I change my ad privacy preferences? (from version 1.2)
If you live in the European Union, the UK or Switzerland, in Settings › Ads and privacy › "Privacy preferences". On iPhone, the tracking permission is changed in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. More details in the privacy policy.
Is my game saved if I change phones?
No. Your game is saved only on the device you play on. Signing in with Apple or Google doesn't copy or sync it, and uninstalling the app erases it.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, Settings › Account › "Delete account". Your account identifier and name are erased from the device; your game stays on the device. To also remove the permission at Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › ArrowUnlace › Delete. At Google: myaccount.google.com/connections › ArrowUnlace › remove the connection.
Do I need a connection?
Not to play. Only to sign in with Apple or Google, which is optional, and, from version 1.2, for ads and the "Remove ads" purchase. Offline there are no ads.
